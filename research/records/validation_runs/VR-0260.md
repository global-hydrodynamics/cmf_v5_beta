## Comparison method and interpretation

Identifiers, field order, record order, categories, sentinels, and string values were compared exactly. Numeric producer outputs were compared with the established reference tolerance derived from the original printed decimal precision. Sample migration used parsed values, so CSV quoting and removal of fixed-width padding do not count as scientific differences.

These checks demonstrate format-contract preservation and behavioral equivalence for the tested Mekong/reference cases. They do not establish independent scientific correctness for every allocation or validate unmodified external maps. Legacy v4 text interfaces remain unchanged.
