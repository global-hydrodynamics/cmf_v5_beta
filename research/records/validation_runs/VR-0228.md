## Performance findings and readiness

Profiling identified avoidable indexed copies and per-cell graph loops.
259200-cell region_set_map drops from~0.99s to~0.20s while preserving integers.
12000²→2400² crop is~1.30s Python vs~1.21s Fortran, with RSS~0.18 vs~5.0GiB.
Large synthetic water aggregation is~6.52 vs~11.99s (RSS~2.4 vs~2.1GiB).
Whole-tile copy improves from~23.4 to~7.0s, still slower than~2.8s Fortran;
RSS is~1.2 vs~4.3GiB. Cut-domain tracing remains a measured optimization target.

21A/2B are workload-scoped candidates, not production approval. Retain Fortran
for throughput-sensitive coarse edge tracing and whole-tile bulk copies pending
review. No global runtime extrapolation, actual3sec scientific integration,
Linux/Windows/big-endian benchmark or model simulation is claimed. Future global
30arcsec coarse maps exceed the current signed-int16 catmx column capacity;
a separately approved format/indexing decision is needed. Large-array residency,
per-basin tables and sequential merging remain scalability considerations.
INPMAT keeps100 slots in three four-byte arrays per coarse cell (~1.0TiB for
43200x21600 before bookkeeping); sparse/block construction is a future task.
