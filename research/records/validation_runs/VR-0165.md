## Limitations and provider policy

[OSMF tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/)
was checked during implementation. Standard OSM is community funded, requires
attribution/normal browser identity/cache/Referer, and is not an unlimited service.
Only visible tiles at one zoom are fetched (bounded32, debounce250ms), never
whole-region/multi-level prefetch. Provider and attribution are environment
configurable; CORS must permit canvas image reads. Requests can fail/offline;
scientific rendering remains local. Online tiles are excluded from PNG rather
than downloaded for export. Basemap output is capped1400px/axis;32-tile limit
may reduce tile detail at large/high-latitude extents. Mercator is unavailable
beyond±85.05112878°; those online rows remain transparent. Native scientific
coordinates remain intact. Browser geographic row resampling has display-pixel
resolution; it does not modify native data or exact click geometry.

Assigned-only percentiles use bounded blocks and disposable disk workspace
proportional to tile size; first computation may pause. Reusable caches assume
read-only source files for the session. Native high-resolution NetCDF layout and
pixelwise fldhgt have not been verified/supplied and are not invented. Matplotlib
and Bokeh typography/antialiasing differ; scientific palettes/limits/geometry are
shared, not an identical browser screenshot export. Old historical DESIGN
sections retain superseded rationale and explicitly defer to DT-0119.
