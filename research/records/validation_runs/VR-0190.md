## Change and boundary

Shared and Mekong s90 copies remain byte-identical. Existing literal variant
mapping validation is done before the selection summary, which reports selected,
existing and new outputs. Interactive overwrite-all y/Y grants force to all;
n/N/Enter asks per existing file. Individual y only grants force to that file.
Invalid answers retry; read EOF is treated as empty/decline, like s02.
Noninteractive existing files are skipped and new files convert; --force or
FORCE=1 bypasses prompts. Converted/skipped totals end the workflow.

No input/reference mapping, converter options beyond force permission, registry,
Binary/NetCDF layout, scientific processing, actual samples or other wrappers
changed. Only temporary fixtures are rewritten. Registry inspected and unchanged.
