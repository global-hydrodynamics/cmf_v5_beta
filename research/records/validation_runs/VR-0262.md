## Interpretation and limitations

The fallback correctly downgrades an incompatible dependency already present in `.venv` and validates the resulting 2024 profile. It intentionally does not remove unrelated packages that conflict with the selected profile; such conflicts are visible through `pip check` and stop setup rather than causing an automatic environment reset. The actual current-standard resolution exposed a Panel UI add-on conflict after fallback, so that specific path needs maintainer review or a separately authorized compatibility policy before it can be claimed as an end-to-end successful setup. No older OS was available; broad OS compatibility remains unverified.
