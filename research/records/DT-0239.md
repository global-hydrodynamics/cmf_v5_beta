## Finding and change

All 425 valid Mekong bifurcation paths exceeded the previous `1e-5°` coordinate
tolerance. The largest longitude and latitude differences were approximately
`0.000420°` and `0.000418°`, respectively, both within the `0.0005°` maximum
introduced by the three-decimal CSV coordinate representation. Converting all
425 CSV `end_lon` and `end_lat` values to float32 exactly reproduced the
corresponding NetCDF values.

The supplementary-coordinate comparison in `f03b` now allows `0.0006°` for
both axes. Index, width, and valid-outlet checks are unchanged. Rendered link
coordinates still come from `lonlat.nc`; no scientific or map data were
modified.
