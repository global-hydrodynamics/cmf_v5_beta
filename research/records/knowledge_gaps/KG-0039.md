# KG-0039 — Lower-Ahr reach representation after catchment allocation

- Gap ID: `KG-0039`
- Linked event ID(s): `EV-0691`
- Category: scientific assumption
- Evidence basis: source-code evidence / unresolved
- Status: open

## What was unknown or assumed?

Does the current Rhine 15-minute routing map resolve the impacted lower-Ahr
reaches as independent tributary channels, or aggregate these locations into a
large receiving-channel unit catchment? A valid catmxy lookup alone cannot answer
the named-reach or flood-wave question. This is non-blocking for Step 21's
candidate handoff but material to future local-flow interpretation.

## Discovery and verified facts

The maintainer requested comparison with the retained geographic allocations.
The restored one-minute catmxy maps Altenahr, Bad Neuenahr, Sinzig, Pegel
Altenahr and Bad Bodendorf to model cell (9,17), uparea 140,560.780 km².
Schuld/Müsch map to (10,15), which drains directly into (9,17). The selected
standard nextxy paths terminate without cycles or area decreases. These are
model-data facts; they do not independently identify the physical reach or
prove absence of an Ahr channel elsewhere. Town-coordinate and pixel-scale
uncertainty also remain. The question was recorded during the current review;
no resolution is claimed.
