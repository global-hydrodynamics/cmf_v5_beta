# KG-0037: Historical climatology provenance and acceptance of grid-only filling

- Gap ID: KG-0037

## Trigger and verified facts

Recorded during the task after source inspection and before interpreting the
real-data discrepancy. The legacy workflow gates donors and targets through an
Input Matrix; the maintainer explicitly requested removing that dependency.
The historical reference's exact original mask, source lineage and complete
normalization/precision chain have not been recovered. Source routines alone
do not reconstruct the separately retained reference Binary.

The new field adds positive runoff at 807 reference-zero cells; the largest
absolute discrepancy is at a reference-zero cell. Originally positive cells
differ by at most 9.4587449e-11 mm/s. This supports changed selection-domain
coverage as the main explanation of large differences, but does not prove an
unavailable historical mask. All 365-day/cell valid-year counts are 30, excluding
missing-year policy as a difference source in this particular run.

## Questions and why they matter

What precise input, mask and arithmetic chain produced the retained reference?
Is filling all grid-only targets, including inland dry cells, appropriate for
adoption in the channel-parameter reference? Minimum filling has no independent
physical-optimality or water-balance validation. The maintainer's instruction
approves implementing/comparing the new method, not silently replacing a
scientifically reviewed reference. Independent tool use/comparison can continue.
