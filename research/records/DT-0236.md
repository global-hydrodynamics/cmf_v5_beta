## Findings and implementation

The current checkout discovered `1min/location.csv` and registered both
`COG/1min/1min.catmxy.tif` and `1min.visual.tif`; no invalid-COG diagnostics
were produced. The reported Missing state could not be reproduced. Discovery
was nevertheless passing a paired NetCDF provenance path under the obsolete
`1min_nc/` directory. It now resolves the NetCDF from the declared resolution
directory, falling back to the actual legacy location directory when needed.

Applying p01-f04b then exposed a separate metadata-precision mismatch. The
supplementary `end_lon` and `end_lat` fields are stored to three decimal places,
while the Explorer compared them against float32 `lonlat` endpoints at a much
tighter tolerance. The comparison now uses the stored three-decimal precision;
one-based destination indices and the `lonlat` product remain authoritative for
the rendered connections. COG checks for CRS, geometry, bands, units, types and
provenance filename were not relaxed.

No scientific raster or COG files were modified. No material new knowledge
gaps were identified.
