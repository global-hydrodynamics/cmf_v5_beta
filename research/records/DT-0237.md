## Decision and change

Development Levels describe the guarantee and scope of a task, not a larger
test quota. L1 establishes that the target function works; L2 examines plausible
effects on related components; L3 establishes the distributed product and its
supported workflows. L0 remains local and follows the root `AGENTS.md` rule
when explicitly designated. Scientific risk independently changes the depth
of evidence needed for the target guarantee, not the breadth of components
investigated. Levels can be selected independently during staged development.

Only the existing Skill and the concise development-status/research records
were updated. No code, test suite, or repository-wide policy was changed.
