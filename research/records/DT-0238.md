## Decision and change

Current visualization paths use the shared Map metadata readers and standard
files: `params.nml`, root-level base-grid NetCDF, high-resolution NetCDF under
the resolution directory, and `location.csv`. The River Map catchment readers
use the registered NetCDF assignment variable names `catmxx` and `catmyy`.
Explicit Explorer TXT/Binary compatibility remains intact.

The change covers River Map, output analysis, the shared flood-depth downscaler,
the visualization catalog, focused test fixtures that pointed at the current
Mekong map, and directly affected READMEs. Published `visualization/gallery/`
assets, map/model data, and scientific rendering methods were not changed.

The p01-f04 workflow has a separate observed `bifprm.nc` destination/outlet
coordinate mismatch after map-format inputs load. This is not a format-reader
issue and was left for a separate scientific-data investigation.
