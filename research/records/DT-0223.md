## Contract

The v4 `READ_BIFPARAM` routine consumes `(ix,iy,jx,jy)`, length, elevation, depth, and `NPTHLEV` ordered widths. The first width is river-channel width; following widths are overland layers. `end_lat` and `end_lon` are v5 supplementary outlet coordinates and are not part of the v4 calculation record.

`s32-meta2txt.sh` now omits and reports those two columns while retaining every v4 field in order. Other populated non-representable fields and invalid row-ordered `pth_id` values still fail before replacing the destination. CSV header keys are normalized to accommodate whitespace around source column names.
