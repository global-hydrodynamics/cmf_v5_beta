# DR-0066 — Limit bifurcation coordinate precision in CSV output

- **Discussion ID:** DR-0066
- **Linked event ID(s):** EV-0828–EV-0830
- **Source type:** Maintainer instruction in active Codex conversation
- **Source reference:** Active Codex conversation, 2026-11-27
- **Source access:** restricted
- **Maintainer review:** pending

## Decision

Write `start_lat`, `start_lon`, `end_lat`, and `end_lon` in `bifprm.csv` to three decimal places for readability. Regenerate the tracked derived Mekong `bifprm.nc` from that CSV so both representations agree. Do not change other parameter values.

## Rationale and effect

This is a requested coordinate precision convention. Rounding to 0.001° can move a stored coordinate by up to 0.0005° (about 56 m in latitude); this affects stored endpoint coordinates and derived NetCDF coordinate values, but not grid indices, path topology, widths, or model hydraulic calculations.

## Outcome

The generator now formats all four geographic coordinate columns to three decimals. The 382-row Mekong CSV and NetCDF were updated. Tests confirm the coordinate precision, NetCDF/CSV agreement, and preservation of all other sample parameter fields.
