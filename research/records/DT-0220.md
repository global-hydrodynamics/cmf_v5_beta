## Scope and changes

Replaced the 2022 historical direct-dependency profile with the exact versions
recorded in the completed Cloud Codex Debian 11 / CPython 3.10.9 / Linux amd64
experiment. Added the corresponding experimental 2020 profile for Debian 10 /
CPython 3.8.7 / Linux amd64. Both files retain the SHA-256-pinned PyPI wheel
URL for `lmoments3` 1.0.4.

Updated `config/python/README.md`, the dependency audit, and the compatibility
handoff to distinguish the official β02 2024 fallback from experimental,
manual-only 2022 and 2020 profiles. The 2020 PyYAML pure-Python installation
procedure and optional `importlib-resources` probe are documented separately.

## Boundaries

The Cloud Docker experiments were not repeated. Direct pins do not lock
transitive dependencies or establish compatibility beyond their tested Linux
environments and scoped checks. Neither historical profile is selected by
`s02-python_setup.sh`. The standard requirements, 2024 profile, setup and
activation scripts, and scientific algorithms were left unchanged.
