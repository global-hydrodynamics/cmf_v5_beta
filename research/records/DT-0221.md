## Scope and behavior

The river-point tool now detects `-9999` missing-value conventions in paired
`nextxy` indices, the basin identifier, and a high-resolution `catmxy`
assignment before attempting calculations. It emits a clear undefined-cell
message and returns normally. Geographic indices outside the map remain parser
errors. Valid river-cell output is preserved. The shared a01 template and its
Mekong and Rhine map-local copies identify the Mekong-only example coordinates.

No scientific calculation, source map data, or coordinate-selection rule was
changed.
