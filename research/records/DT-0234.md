## Scope and implementation

`discover_tiles()` already searched `location.csv` before this task and skipped
a sibling TXT inventory when CSV existed. The CSV rows are now parsed through
Map's shared `read_location_csv()` contract rather than a second local
`DictReader` implementation. CSV remains authoritative; legacy TXT discovery
remains available for older Binary-only maps. README and DESIGN describe this
precedence and compatibility boundary.

No raster data or rendering behavior was changed. The reported Gallery
availability was not runtime-verified under L0; user functional validation
remains necessary. No material new knowledge gaps were found.
